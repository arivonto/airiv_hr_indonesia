AIRIV Indonesia People Operations

Indonesia HR & Employee Operations Engine

Indonesian HR workspace for Odoo 18 with employee lifecycle, organization structure, attendance context, leave administration, and payroll-ready operational records.

Odoo 18 CommunityHRIndonesiaEmployee LifecycleAIRIV

Core Capabilities

AIRIV HR Indonesia connects people, organization, attendance, leave, and employment records into a clear operational layer for Indonesian teams.

People Registry

Employee lifecycle

Maintain employee identity, employment status, department, position, contract, and responsible manager context.

Workforce Operations

Attendance and leave

Keep attendance, leave requests, approvals, and team availability connected to employee records.

Payroll Readiness

Structured handoff

Prepare clean employee and work-activity context for payroll and Indonesian HR review workflows.

Feature & Workflow Automation

1

Set organization

Configure departments, positions, work locations, managers, and employment policies.

2

Register employee

Create employee records, contracts, identity details, and lifecycle status.

3

Run operations

Record attendance, submit leave requests, and process approval responsibilities.

4

Review payroll context

Validate employee, contract, attendance, and leave data before payroll handoff.

Clone branch 18.0, place the module on the addons path, restart Odoo, update the Apps list, and install.
